# Homepage visual skeleton

Goal: a homepage visual skeleton for a high-risk payment processor. No database and no business logic.

## Sections

- Navigation
- Hero: left placeholders and a Merchant Health Engine dashboard mock on the right
- Trust metrics band
- Problem and education area with diagram and flow shells
- Industry matrix with abstract card graphics
- Health diagnosis controls with no calculation
- Merchant Academy cards left empty until real content exists
- Process and footer

## Palette and type

Clear-sky white and morning-mist white backgrounds. Lifted deep navy and Prussian blue, not near-black. Emerald and jade only for health, trends, and key feedback. Fine rules, light shadow, data graphics, and generous whitespace. The tone should feel steady and grown. Desktop and mobile must stay uncrowded.

## Scope

Homepage visuals plus global color and type. Add a page title and a share description. Keep visible marketing copy out until content is approved.
